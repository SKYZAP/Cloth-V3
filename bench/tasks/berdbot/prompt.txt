Add three queue-editing slash commands to berdbot's music commands: /remove, /move and /shuffle.

Put each in its own file next to the existing music commands, registered the same way (module.exports with `data` and `execute`, default member permissions):
- src/modules/discord/commands/music/remove.command.ts
- src/modules/discord/commands/music/move.command.ts
- src/modules/discord/commands/music/shuffle.command.ts

Positions are 1-based and count the upcoming tracks exactly as /queue numbers them: position 1 is the next track to play, and the current track is not counted.

/remove position:<integer, required, minimum 1>
- Removes the upcoming track at that position.
- Reply: `> **Removed** <title> **from the queue.**`

/move from:<integer, required, minimum 1> to:<integer, required, minimum 1>
- Moves the upcoming track at `from` so it ends up at position `to`; the other tracks keep their order.
- Reply: `> **Moved** <title> **to position <to>.**`
- If `from` and `to` are the same, change nothing and reply (ephemeral): `That track is already at position <to>.`

/shuffle (no options)
- Shuffles the upcoming tracks once, right away. The current track is not affected, and it does not turn on a lasting shuffle mode.
- Reply: `> **Shuffled <n> tracks.**` where n is the number of upcoming tracks.
- With fewer than 2 upcoming tracks, change nothing and reply (ephemeral): `There aren't enough tracks in the queue to shuffle.`

Errors (all ephemeral; nothing changes):
- No queue for the server: `This server does not have an active player session.`
- A position outside the queue (for /remove, or `from` or `to` in /move; for /move check `from` first): `There is no track at position <n>. The queue has <m> upcoming tracks.`
- Any unexpected error: log it and reply `Couldn’t change the queue. Please try again.`, unless the interaction was already replied to.

Ephemeral means `flags: MessageFlags.Ephemeral`.
